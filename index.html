Club Hub

This website started out as a class project under the original name Clubba Hubba. It is designed to help student clubs at my school improve their outreach and planning within our school community. Three of the main things the website does is allow club leaders to create a club page, compete to get the most points (earned by having meetings and high attendance), and schedule meetings that will then be displayed on the website's calendar. There are also various other things including a page for the dean of students and other site administrators, however, these pages are not accessible to general users

Club Hub was built with HTML, Javascript, and CSS
